{\sffamily\bfseries\color{IEEEorange!75!black}DID YOU KNOW?}\quad
The fraction $355/113$, associated with Zu Chongzhi in the fifth century, matches $\pi$ to six decimal places. Robert Recorde introduced the equals sign $=$ in 1557, while John Wallis introduced $\infty$ in 1655.
