  \midrule
  Sensitivity & Measures the electrical response to the target condition & Raw current, reference current, bias point, and complete calculation \\
  Hysteresis & Reveals trap-related disagreement between forward and reverse sweeps & Sweep direction, rate, current criterion, and extracted $HW$ \\
  Stability & Shows whether calibration survives temperature and repeated operation & Temperature series, cycling, drift, and ageing measurements \\
  Interface Quality & Determines trap density and reproducibility & Process details, passivation, extracted $N_{it}$, and device variation \\
  Biological Validation & Establishes whether the electrical contrast is clinically meaningful & Receptors, controls, liquid environment, noise, and blinded samples \\
  \bottomrule

\medskip
{\sffamily\bfseries\color{IEEEblue}KEY RULE}\quad
Always report sensitivity and reliability together; a large fresh-device signal can hide serious trap-induced detection error.
