{\sffamily\bfseries\color{IEEEorange!75!black}DID YOU KNOW?}\quad
In this simulation, fixed cell charge shifts the threshold voltage but does not itself create hysteresis. Switching traps do: slowing the gate sweep from $1$ to $0.01\,\mathrm{V/s}$ increases the predicted T47D hysteresis from $10.2$ to $32.8\,\mathrm{mV}$. The damaged sensor also shows nearly $97\%$ sensitivity inaccuracy for the healthy MCF-10A condition.
