\small\RaggedRight \textbf{\color{IEEEblue!75!black}Did you know?} ---{\footnotesize\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em,itemsep=1pt,topsep=1pt,parsep=0pt,partopsep=0pt]
\item The global average cost of a data breach reached USD~4.88 million in IBM's 2024 report, a 10\% jump in a single year.
\item Organizations using AI and automation extensively in prevention workflows saved about USD~1.88 million per breach on average.
\item The mean time to identify and contain a breach fell to 258 days, a seven-year low from 277 days the year before.
\item Compromised credentials were the entry point in 16\% of breaches, and those attacks took 292 days to identify and contain.
\item Forty percent of breaches involved data spread across multiple environments, averaging over USD~5 million and 283 days.
\item Microsoft's research indicates that multi-factor authentication blocks more than 99\% of account compromise attacks.
\item Microsoft's telemetry shows that more than 99.9\% of compromised accounts did not have multi-factor authentication enabled.
\item Verizon's 2024 DBIR found that 68\% of breaches involve a non-malicious human element, such as an error or a phishing mistake.
\item Vulnerability exploitation as the initial access step nearly tripled in that report, rising by about 180\% in a year.
\item Ransomware or extortion featured in 32\% of breaches, and third-party involvement rose 68\% to 15\%.
\item Stolen credentials have appeared in almost one-third of breaches over the past decade.
\item Malicious insider attacks averaged USD~4.99 million, the costliest initial vector in that report.
\item Healthcare remained the costliest industry at USD~9.77 million, although its average fell by 10.6\%.
\item The United States recorded the highest average cost at USD~9.36 million for the 14th consecutive year.
\item Only 12\% of breached organizations fully recovered, and most recoveries took more than 100 days.
\item Ransomware was a top threat across 92\% of industries in the 2024 DBIR.
\item Half of the breaches studied in EMEA were internal rather than external.
\item Sixty-seven percent of organizations deployed security AI and automation, a near 10\% jump, and 20\% used some form of generative AI security tool.
\item Organizations using those technologies extensively detected and contained incidents on average 98 days faster.
\item TLS~1.3 removed static RSA key exchange, so all public-key exchanges now provide forward secrecy.
\end{enumerate}}
